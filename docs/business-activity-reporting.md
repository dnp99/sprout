# Business activity reporting

Businesses let someone distinguish activity for a side business without
changing their personal budget categories or income sources. A business is an
optional label that can be applied to either an income or expense transaction.

## Managing businesses

Settings → **Businesses** creates and manages the labels. Each has a name,
emoji, and accent colour. Renaming a business keeps every existing assignment.
Deleting a business preserves its transactions and clears their business
assignment, so transaction history and budget totals are never lost.

## Assigning a business

The Add Transaction and Edit Transaction forms on desktop and mobile include an
optional **Business** picker. It is intentionally separate from:

- **Categories**, which describe an expense's budget purpose.
- **Income sources**, which describe where positive income came from.

For example, a Yoga class payment can be both `Income source: Side business`
and `Business: Yoga`; studio rent can be `Category: Rent` and `Business: Yoga`.
This keeps the personal budget accurate while retaining a future profit-and-loss
view for the business.
